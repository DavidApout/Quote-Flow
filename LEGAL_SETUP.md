# QuoteFlow demo legal information

Public routes (no sign-in required): `/legal/privacy`, `/legal/terms`, `/legal/demo`.
A shared footer appears on account, customer, widget/private-link, payment-return,
and legal screens. Privacy/terms notices are next to account registration and
customer submission; payment information is linked next to checkout.
Links open in a new tab so reading the notice does not discard a draft or expose
a private request token. Legal pages contain no account or request data.

Public contact supplied by the owner: officalauraslides@gmail.com.
Location supplied: United States. Public fields and document version live in
`src/lib/legal.js`. QuoteFlow is identified as a demo project, not an invented
registered business. No state or governing-law clause was assumed.

These are demo disclosures, not attorney-reviewed production legal documents.
No consent checkbox or claim of recorded contract acceptance has been added.
No marketing trackers or fake cookie-consent banner has been introduced.
The privacy notice describes current storage, optional AI sharing, sandbox
payments, live-vs-preview mail, access controls and the lack of automatic deletion.

Before a public launch, confirm the legal operator/business name and state,
target jurisdictions and users, counsel-reviewed commercial terms and lawful
processing basis where applicable. Set and implement retention/deletion and
privacy-request handling, backup disposal, incident response and hosting details.
Review image-abuse/copyright reporting and removal obligations, provider terms,
state privacy obligations, and any cookie-consent needs after adding trackers.
Implement versioned server-side acceptance records if production terms require
assent; do not confuse a privacy notice with consent to every data use.
Define real refund/cancellation/tax/fulfillment rules before enabling live payments.
The public contact mailbox must be monitored; no email was sent during this work.

Reference guidance checked November 10, 2026:
- FTC Consumer Privacy: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/consumer-privacy
- FTC Protecting Personal Information: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business
- FTC digital disclosures: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2013/03/ftc-staff-revises-online-advertising-disclosure-guidelines

The FTC guidance informed plain, visible disclosures that match the implementation.
It does not establish that QuoteFlow satisfies every applicable law.
